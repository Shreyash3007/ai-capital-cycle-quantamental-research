# Decision log

This file records durable project choices and the reason behind them. New decisions should be dated and should state what evidence could cause reconsideration.

## 2026-09-23

### Public learning repository

**Decision:** publish this project under the personal GitHub account `Shreyash3007`, with planning, code, session records, evidence, progress, and knowledge links in the same repository. Keep the course PDFs local and excluded from Git.

**Reason:** the public history should show how the learner built and understood the system without redistributing course material or treating documentation as implementation proof.

### Early later-course concepts

**Decision:** later-course methods may be introduced before the learner completes those weeks if the mentor explains the prerequisites from first principles, the learner can work through a bounded task, and evidence records the result.

**Reason:** the research project should not be artificially limited by course order, while course-completion and demonstrated-capability claims remain accurate.

### Mentor Mode and course boundary

**Decision:** Mentor Mode is active. The learner reports completing the whole of C1W2. Later methods may be introduced early with first-principles teaching and independent checks; their course weeks are marked complete only when the learner confirms them. The mentor designs tasks and gives graduated help while the learner writes, runs, explains, and defends assignment code.

**Reason:** course coverage should unlock practice, while project mastery must come from observed independent evidence. Task, phase, and overall completion are tracked in `PROGRESS.md`.

### Quantamental engine identity

**Decision:** rename the project to the AI Capital Cycle Quantamental Research Engine.

**Reason:** the research subject is the economic chain surrounding AI, not a loose collection of companies that market themselves as AI businesses.

### Output-first product

**Decision:** the primary products are versioned data, tested signals, model evidence, company and segment research, event studies, fragility reports, and paper-portfolio attribution. A dashboard is optional.

**Reason:** professional reviewers evaluate the research chain and economic value, not the polish of a viewing surface.

### Segmented universe

**Decision:** segment public companies by their role in the AI capital cycle and treat private frontier labs as ecosystem nodes.

**Reason:** a semiconductor supplier, hyperscaler, and subscription-software company have different accounting, valuation, and causal mechanisms and should not be forced into one naive model.

### Professional review bar

**Decision:** every mature artifact must withstand hedge-fund fundamental, quant-research, AI-finance engineering, and MFE mathematical review.

**Reason:** this standard forces finance, statistics, programming, AI evaluation, reproducibility, and oral defense to develop together.

## 2026-09-22

### Standalone workspace

**Decision:** `D:\ML-project` is the canonical project root.

**Reason:** the project needs complete context and operating instructions without depending on Learning OS.

### Learner-owned code

**Decision:** the learner writes every assignment-code line by hand.

**Reason:** the project must demonstrate executable Python and ML understanding, not code-generation ability.

### Project thesis

**Decision:** build a specialized AI-equity intelligence and bubble-fragility research platform.

**Reason:** it joins the learner's main interests in AI, finance, ML, and later deep learning while providing progressively harder real-world problems.

### First model

**Decision:** start with a from-scratch valuation-stretch regression using comparable-scale synthetic ratios.

**Reason:** it directly uses linear regression, cost, gradient descent, multiple features, and vectorization without requiring unlearned preprocessing or unreliable live data.

### Static-to-live sequence

**Decision:** synthetic data, then frozen historical snapshots, then live adapters.

**Reason:** model understanding and reproducibility must be established before operational complexity.

### Bubble output

**Decision:** preserve separate dimensions, pro and counter-evidence, triggers, and confidence; do not predict an exact burst date.

**Reason:** bubble definitions are contested, labels are imperfect, and exact timing precision would be misleading.

### AI boundary

**Decision:** deterministic code owns calculations; ML owns evaluated prediction; TypeSafe/Jev owns narrow typed text judgments; a generative model owns constrained explanation; humans own high-stakes conclusions.

**Reason:** each component should do work suited to its observable strengths and failure modes.

### Live use

**Decision:** the default live outcome is live data plus a live paper portfolio.

**Reason:** real-money execution introduces a separate authorization, safety, brokerage, and operational-risk problem.

### One-week scope

**Decision:** the original seven-day target applies to the C1W2 foundation, not the entire platform; completion follows evidence even if the schedule changes.

**Reason:** depth requires implementation, failure diagnosis, visualization, and transfer evidence.
