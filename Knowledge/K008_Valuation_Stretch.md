# K008: Valuation stretch

**Course coverage:** project finance concept. **Project evidence:** none yet.

Valuation stretch is the difference between an observed log valuation multiple and a model-estimated log multiple. It shows disagreement with a chosen model. It can reflect speculation, omitted quality, accounting differences, or model error, so it is a research question rather than a bubble verdict.

**Requires:** [linear regression](K001_Linear_Regression.md), [cost and residual analysis](K002_Cost_Function.md), and a sound [finance model](../FINANCE_RESEARCH_MODEL.md). **Used by:** [P00 T02 and T06](../PHASE_00_C1W2_PLAN.md). **Extends to:** [bubble dimensions](../BUBBLE_MONITOR.md).

**Demonstration gate:** calculate and interpret residuals, name alternative explanations, and show why the small synthetic fixture supports no investment claim. Link the eventual evidence here.
