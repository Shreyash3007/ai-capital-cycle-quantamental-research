# Finance research model

## The economic chain

The platform studies AI equities through a connected chain rather than isolated indicators:

```text
AI adoption and demand
    -> revenue and pricing power
    -> margins and cash generation
    -> reinvestment and capital requirements
    -> financing and dilution risk
    -> expected future cash flows
    -> valuation under discount-rate assumptions
    -> market positioning and price dynamics
    -> fragility under a catalyst or liquidity shock
```

A strong project must understand every arrow well enough to state when it may fail.

## Fundamental layer

The company model should eventually distinguish:

- reported revenue from AI-attributed revenue;
- growth caused by volume, price, mix, acquisition, or accounting effects;
- gross margin from operating margin and free-cash-flow margin;
- stock-based compensation from cash expense and dilution;
- maintenance investment from growth investment;
- contracted demand from management aspiration;
- customer concentration from diversified adoption;
- current profitability from terminal-value dependence.

The initial five C1W2 features are teaching variables, not a complete valuation model.

## Valuation layer

No single multiple is universally correct. The mature platform may compare:

- enterprise value to sales for businesses whose earnings are not yet stable;
- enterprise value to EBITDA where operating earnings are meaningful;
- price to earnings where accounting earnings are positive and comparable;
- price or enterprise value to free cash flow where cash conversion is credible;
- discounted cash-flow scenarios for assumption sensitivity;
- peer and own-history percentiles, with explicit peer rules.

Negative or near-zero denominators, share dilution, lease treatment, net cash, and cyclicality must be handled explicitly.

## Valuation stretch

The first model estimates a transformed valuation multiple from fundamentals. Its residual is:

```text
stretch_i = observed_log_multiple_i - predicted_log_multiple_i
```

This residual may reflect speculation, omitted quality, accounting differences, market power, measurement error, or a model that is simply wrong. It becomes useful only when studied with diagnostics and additional evidence.

## Price and market layer

Later measures may include returns, momentum, volatility, turnover, liquidity, drawdown, concentration, and explosive-price statistics. These describe market behavior. They do not automatically explain why it happened.

## Capital-cycle layer

AI markets may involve large infrastructure spending, scarce compute, power constraints, customer prepayments, vendor financing, debt issuance, and equity dilution. The system should trace who funds capacity, who captures margins, and which assumptions depend on abundant capital.

## Scenario layer

Research should identify conditional pathways:

- fundamentals catch up while price stays stable;
- valuation compresses gradually;
- a catalyst causes rapid repricing;
- liquidity stress amplifies a moderate disappointment;
- counter-evidence strengthens and invalidates the bubble thesis.

Scenario analysis is not a promise that one path will occur.

## Portfolio layer

Company analysis and portfolio construction are different problems. A strong company thesis can still create a poor portfolio when exposure, correlation, liquidity, turnover, or timing is uncontrolled. Portfolio research begins only after signals have honest out-of-sample evidence.

## Questions every result must answer

1. What economic mechanism could produce this signal?
2. What alternative explanation exists?
3. What information was available at the time?
4. Which assumptions dominate the conclusion?
5. What evidence would invalidate it?
6. Does the result survive costs, concentration, and a different period?
7. Is the output a measurement, forecast, scenario, or judgment?
